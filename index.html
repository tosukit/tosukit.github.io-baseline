Three findings from the research

Drawn from McKinsey, Harvard Business Review and peer-reviewed studies.

Demand is growing

Large companies see AI as a competitive advantage and are extending outsourcing deals to include AI tools. That creates demand BPOs can serve.

Personalization needs good data

AI can tailor service, but only on well-integrated, responsibly handled data.

PrivacyBiasTransparency

AI works best beside people

In a study of thousands of support agents, an AI assistant raised productivity, most for newer agents.

Customers more politeFewer manager requests

What Foundever should do

Four recommendations. Open one for details.

1. Prioritize AI that assists agents
How
Start with agent-assist tools in a few client programs.
Risk
Results from one setting may not carry over.
Measure
Resolution time, customer satisfaction and escalation rates against similar programs.
2. Build personalization on omnichannel data, with safeguards
How
Use connected phone, email, chat and social data, with clear rules on what is used and how.
Risk
Privacy failures or biased outputs that damage trust.
Measure
Complaint and opt-out rates, and service quality across customer groups.
3. Develop its leaders
How
AI training for managers and client-facing leaders, tied to the pilots.
Measure
Share of leaders who can interpret AI performance data and act on it.
4. Redesign roles around human-AI collaboration
How
Involve agents in designing workflows; keep humans for complex and empathetic cases.
Risk
More pressure on agents if productivity targets rise unchecked.
Measure
Agent satisfaction, turnover and workload over time.
Use roughly 40 years of experience to invest in AI that assists human agents, not replaces them.
